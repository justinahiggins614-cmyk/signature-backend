● HOW THE MODES USE IT

  • System AIs — call chat() with precise intent rules; runDemo() runs their instrument panels.
  • Persona AIs — call chat() with dramatic first-person rule replies; demos run their themed mini-labs.
  • Domain AIs — call chat() with expert-helpful rules; demos run checklists, calculators and guided walkthroughs.
  • Mix Lab — blends two AI records, then hands the hybrid to chat() and runDemo() like any other mode.
  • Dial-up calls — dial(aiRecord) opens a live session: the AI answers with its greeting, say(text) chats with call-duration memory, runDemo(inputs) runs its demo, hangup() closes the line. session.lockRole('semiconductor replacement') locks the AI into a role for the whole call — replies turn concise, in-role, machine-friendly (short structured ROLE / ACK / OUT / END lines, no wandering, no breaking character), suitable for driving automation; session.unlockRole() releases it back to normal conversation. Try 'auto-pen' too.
  • Preset options — presets() lists the 10 one-tap archetypes; loadPreset(name) returns the full directory-grade record (JAH-AI-PRE-001…010), ready to dial.
  • The Opperater — geneOptions(advanced) lists the little gene boxes; the operator drops boxes into the big box any number of times (repeats strengthen the trait), sets the four attitude sliders, then genomeViable(drops) announces when the mix is a valid interactable AI — buildGenome({drops, attitude, name, tone, mission}) forges the record and the operator plugs the call: dial(forged). fileRecord(rec) files a new AI into the book: stamps it JAH-AI-OP-###### and returns {record, downloadPy, downloadJson} — the filed AI is immediately dialable.
